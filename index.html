⚙️ Technology Behind It

We use the powerful LaserPecker 4, a dual-laser engraving machine designed for precision and portability.

How the Machine Works

The LaserPecker 4 uses two types of lasers:

  • Diode Laser – Ideal for organic materials like leaves, wood, and leather
  • Infrared Laser – Used for metals and certain plastics

For leaves, the machine uses controlled heat to burn micro-details onto the surface. This process creates a contrast between light and dark areas, forming a clear image.

Step-by-Step Process

  1. Photo is captured digitally
  2. Image is converted into engraving format
  3. Laser scans line-by-line at high precision
  4. Image is permanently engraved onto leaf

The result is a high-detail, long-lasting image embedded directly into a natural material.
